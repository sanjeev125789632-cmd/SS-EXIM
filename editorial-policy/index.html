Editorial Policy

How SS Exim prepares, reviews and corrects general technical website content.

Purpose and scope

Website articles provide general educational guidance about electrical distribution, panels, automation and building-management systems. They do not replace a site survey, approved design, manufacturer documentation, applicable regulations or project-specific assessment by a qualified electrical engineer.

Sources and technical claims

Technical claims should be supported by primary sources such as current IEC publications, BIS/IS records, applicable regulations or official manufacturer documentation. A reference to a standard does not claim that a particular SS Exim product or project complies with that standard. Product compliance is published only when evidence for the exact product is available.

Authors and review

Where an individual author or technical reviewer has not been verified, content is attributed to the “SS-EXIM Technical Team.” SS Exim does not claim review by a qualified engineer unless the reviewer’s identity, role, relevant qualifications and permission to publish have been confirmed.

Dates and updates

Articles display a publication date when it can be verified from the site record. The updated date changes only when the article receives a substantive content revision. Articles are reviewed and updated when verified technical information, manufacturer documentation or applicable requirements change.

Corrections

Readers can report a possible error through the contact page, by emailing info@ss-exim.com, or by calling +91 70451 85111. Correction requests should identify the page, statement and supporting primary source.

Commercial and engineering limitations

Prices, savings, equipment selection, performance and project results depend on verified inputs and an agreed scope. Website content must not be used as a quotation, warranty, design approval or guarantee of performance.
