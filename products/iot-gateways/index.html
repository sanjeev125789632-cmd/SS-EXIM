Tor Industrial IoT Connectivity Gateways

Edge telemetry hardware range — Tor Jupiter, Tor Venus, Tor Pluto, and Tor Mars — linking power meters, PLCs, and environmental sensors to central cloud dashboards.

Tor.AI Channel Partner

Bridging Physical Electrical Gear to Digital Cloud Intelligence

Industrial IoT connectivity relies on robust, secure edge hardware capable of operating in noisy electrical environments. The Tor Gateway Family—comprising the Tor Jupiter, Tor Venus, Tor Pluto, and Tor Mars—serves as the secure communication bridge between field sensors, switchgear panels, and cloud analytics servers.

Supplied and integrated across India by SS Exim, these gateways poll Modbus RTU registers from energy meters (Tor Titan), gather analog/digital signals from BMS sensors, perform edge filtering, and securely transmit encrypted data via Cellular 4G/LTE, Wi-Fi, or Ethernet.

Explore the Tor Gateway Lineup

Tor Jupiter Gateway

Enterprise flagship gateway with multi-port RS485, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and 4G LTE. Ideal for central LT control rooms and high-density meter networks.

Tor Venus Gateway

Compact DIN-rail gateway optimized for OEM panels, APFC boards, and sub-metering nodes requiring reliable Modbus-to-Cloud conversion.

Tor Pluto & Mars Gateways

Edge telemetry gateways engineered for localized sensor polling, wireless telemetry aggregation, and battery-assisted backup reporting.

Frequently Asked Questions — Tor IoT Gateways

What is the difference between Tor Jupiter, Venus, and Pluto?

Tor Jupiter is the multi-interface enterprise gateway for central plant networks. Tor Venus is a compact DIN-rail gateway for individual electrical panels. Tor Pluto and Mars offer localized wireless telemetry for remote sensor points.

Do these gateways work with third-party energy meters?

Yes. In addition to Tor Titan meters, Tor gateways can poll any standard RS485 Modbus RTU energy meter, VFD, or PLC from manufacturers like Schneider, Siemens, ABB, or L&T.

What happens if internet connectivity drops temporarily?

Tor gateways feature onboard flash memory buffering, storing telemetry data locally during internet outages and auto-syncing with the cloud once connection is restored.
