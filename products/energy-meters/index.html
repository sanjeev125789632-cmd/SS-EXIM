Tor Titan Smart Multifunction Energy Meter (PMU / MFM)

Industrial power monitoring unit capturing ~68 electrical parameters with high precision for energy audit, power quality assessment, and cloud BMS integration.

Tor.AI Product Integration

Precision Energy Telemetry & Power Quality Analytics

The Tor Titan is a high-performance smart multifunction energy meter (MFM) and Power Monitoring Unit (PMU) engineered specifically for industrial power distribution and IBMS telemetry. Unlike basic kWh meters, Tor Titan continuously measures approximately 68 electrical parameters across 3-phase and 1-phase electrical networks.

By capturing real-time voltage unbalance, current harmonics (THD), power factor, frequency fluctuations, and active/reactive energy demand, the Tor Titan empowers plant managers and facility engineers to identify power losses, avoid utility penalty surcharges, and prevent transformer overloads.

Frequently Asked Questions — Tor Titan Meters

How many electrical parameters does Tor Titan measure?

Tor Titan measures approximately 68 electrical parameters, including phase voltages, line currents, active power (kW), reactive power (kVAr), apparent power (kVA), power factor, frequency, total harmonic distortion (THD), and energy registers.

Is Tor Titan suitable for industrial APFC panels?

Yes. Due to its precise power factor and harmonic distortion monitoring, Tor Titan is ideal for APFC (Automatic Power Factor Correction) panels, allowing real-time capacitor bank switching control.

What communication protocols are supported?

Tor Titan supports Modbus RTU over RS485, Wi-Fi, BLE, and Ethernet, enabling direct connection to Tor IoT gateways, third-party PLCs, or SCADA software.
