BMS & Industrial IoT Sensor Solutions

Comprehensive sensor portfolio — Tor Mercury (Temperature), Tor Neptune (Vibration), Tor Ceres (Fuel Level), Humidity, Occupancy, Air Quality, and Energy sensors — supplied and integrated across India.

Tor.AI Product Supply

Accurate Physical Telemetry for Industrial Automation

Reliable automation depends entirely on sensor accuracy. SS Exim supplies a full suite of specialized IoT and BMS sensors engineered for high precision, low drift, and long service life in demanding industrial environments.

From monitoring ambient room conditions to tracking motor bearings and diesel generator fuel levels, our sensor range connects seamlessly with Tor Saturn controllers, Tor gateways, and central IBMS software.

Specialized Tor Sensor Lineup

Tor.AI Specialized Field Sensors

Tor Mercury — Temperature

High Precision Sensing

Duct, immersion, and ambient room temperature sensors designed for cleanrooms, server rooms, and AHU air streams.

Tor Neptune — Vibration

Predictive Maintenance

Tri-axial vibration and accelerometer sensor for motors, chilled water pumps, and cooling tower fans. Detects bearing wear early.

Tor Ceres — Fuel Level

Generator Diesel Telemetry

Precision capacitive fuel level transmitter for diesel generator day-tanks and bulk storage, preventing dry-runs during outages.

Environmental & Air Quality BMS Sensors

Standard field sensors for commercial building automation.

Relative Humidity (RH)

Duct and wall-mount RH sensors (20%–95% RH range) for AHUs and HVAC humdification control.

Indoor Air Quality (IAQ)

CO2, TVOC, and PM2.5 sensors for demand-controlled ventilation in office towers.

PIR Occupancy Sensors

Ceiling-mount motion and occupancy detectors for automated office lighting and AC setpoint setback.

Differential Pressure (DP)

Cleanroom and HEPA filter clogging monitors detecting static pressure drops across filter banks.

Frequently Asked Questions — BMS & IoT Sensors

What specialized sensors are available in the Tor family?

Key specialized sensors include Tor Mercury (high-precision temperature), Tor Neptune (vibration and motor diagnostics), and Tor Ceres (diesel generator fuel level monitoring).

Are Tor sensors compatible with non-Tor BMS controllers?

Yes. Because Tor sensors provide standard industrial outputs (4-20mA, 0-10V, PT1000, and RS485 Modbus), they integrate with any standard BMS controller or PLC.

Does SS Exim supply thermowells and mounting accessories?

Yes. SS Exim supplies stainless steel thermowells for pipe immersion sensors, duct mounting flanges, and outdoor weather shields.
