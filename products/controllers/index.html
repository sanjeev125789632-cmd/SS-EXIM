Tor Saturn Programmable Controllers

Smart Direct Digital Controllers (DDC) — Saturn AHU Controller and Saturn Universal Controller — engineered for precision HVAC automation, VFD fan control, and industrial process loops.

Tor.AI Product Supply

Precision Climate & Automation Control

The Tor Saturn Controller Series provides high-density input/output (I/O) control for building management and HVAC systems. Available in dedicated Saturn AHU Controller and Saturn Universal Controller variants, these units execute localized closed-loop PID control for temperature, relative humidity, air static pressure, and motorized valve positions.

Integrated by SS Exim into custom panel enclosures across Mumbai, Pune, and Bangalore, Tor Saturn controllers connect directly to return air temperature sensors (Tor Mercury), differential pressure transmitters, VFD fan drives, and Modbus networks.

Controller Variants

Tor Saturn Family Options

Tor Saturn AHU Controller

Dedicated HVAC Automation

Specifically programmed for Air Handling Unit (AHU) automation. Includes dedicated routines for supply fan VFD modulation, chilled water 2-way / 3-way valve positioning, fresh air damper control, and dirty filter alarm differential sensing.

  • ✓ Closed-loop PID temperature & humidity control
  • ✓ VFD fan speed modulation via 0-10V / 4-20mA
  • ✓ Modbus RTU interface for IBMS 4.0 integration

Tor Saturn Universal Controller

Flexible Multi-Loop DDC

A general-purpose programmable DDC controller with customizable Analog Inputs (AI), Digital Inputs (DI), Analog Outputs (AO), and Relay Digital Outputs (DO). Ideal for exhaust ventilation, pump sequencing, and custom industrial automation.

  • ✓ User-configurable I/O mappings
  • ✓ Standalone fallback logic for high uptime
  • ✓ Direct connection to BMS sensors array

Frequently Asked Questions — Tor Saturn Controllers

What are the primary variants of the Tor Saturn Controller?

The Tor Saturn family includes the Saturn AHU Controller (dedicated to Air Handling Unit fan speed, valve, and filter control) and the Saturn Universal Controller (flexible DDC controller for general building automation).

Can Tor Saturn controllers operate independently without a central server?

Yes. Saturn controllers store local PID logic in non-volatile flash memory, enabling autonomous AHU and valve regulation even if central BMS server connection is lost.

Does SS Exim supply complete pre-wired control panels?

Yes, SS Exim custom fabricates LT control panels containing Tor Saturn controllers, circuit protection, VFDs, and relays for easy plug-and-play field installation.
