Integrated Building Management System (IBMS / BMS 4.0)

Enterprise-grade BMS 4.0 centralized automation for HVAC, lighting, energy tracking, and safety monitoring, supplied and integrated by SS Exim as an official Tor.AI channel partner.

Tor.AI Channel Partner

Centralized Intelligence for Modern Infrastructure

An Integrated Building Management System (IBMS) serves as the central brain of modern commercial buildings, industrial complexes, and data centers. By unifying HVAC plant controls, energy sub-metering, indoor air quality (IAQ) sensors, lighting automation, and equipment telemetry into a single browser-based dashboard, IBMS 4.0 drastically cuts operational expenditure and carbon emissions.

As an official channel partner for Tor.AI, SS Exim designs, supplies, installs, and services complete BMS 4.0 architectures. We integrate Tor Jupiter edge gateways, Tor Titan smart multifunction energy meters, Tor Saturn programmable controllers, and environmental sensor arrays tailored to your building's specific thermal and power loads.

Frequently Asked Questions — IBMS & BMS 4.0

Do you provide turn-key IBMS installation and commissioning?

Yes. SS Exim handles complete turn-key projects: system architecture design, control panel fabrication, sensor wiring, gateway programming, software dashboard setup, commissioning, and maintenance across Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, and pan-India.

Can Tor.AI BMS 4.0 integrate with existing legacy HVAC or switchgear?

Yes. Tor.AI gateways support open protocols including Modbus RTU/TCP, BACnet, and MQTT, enabling seamless integration with legacy chillers, VFDs, AHUs, and Schneider/ABB/Siemens LT panels.

What energy savings can be achieved with IBMS automation?

Commercial facilities typically achieve 15% to 30% reduction in electricity bills through automated AHU scheduling, chiller optimization, power factor monitoring, and occupancy-driven lighting control.
