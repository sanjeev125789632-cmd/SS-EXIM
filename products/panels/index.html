Custom Panel Engineering

Tailored Low-Voltage Power Distribution & Control Switchboards

SS Exim is a leading electrical panel builder serving industrial plants, commercial high-rises, IT parks, and infrastructure projects across Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, and pan-India. We engineer and manufacture custom LT switchboards using heavy-gauge CNC sheet metal, high-conductivity EC-grade copper/aluminum busbars, and switchgear selected to the approved project specification from Schneider Electric, ABB, Siemens, Havells, L&T, and NOARK.

Every panel is wired according to stringent electrical safety norms, featuring compartmentalized designs, IP-rated enclosures, clear phase identification, and pre-integrated Tor Titan energy meters or IBMS telemetry modules.

Main Distribution & Sub-Distribution Panels

Primary Low Voltage (LV) distribution switchboards designed to receive incoming power from transformers or diesel generators and distribute it to downstream sub-feeders. Outfitted with high-capacity Air Circuit Breakers (ACB) and Moulded Case Circuit Breakers (MCCB).

  • ✓ Form 3b / Form 4 compartmentalized isolation options
  • ✓ High short-circuit fault withstand capacity rating
  • ✓ Integrated digital metering & busbar temperature monitoring

Motor Control Center (MCC) Panels

Custom MCC panels built to centralize electric motor control in manufacturing facilities, water treatment plants, and process lines. Configured with Direct-On-Line (DOL) starters, Star-Delta starters, Soft Starters, and Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs).

  • ✓ Fully draw-out or fixed feeder compartment options
  • ✓ Schneider / ABB / Siemens contactors & overload relays
  • ✓ Integrated BMS / PLC speed modulation interfaces

Automatic Power Factor Correction (APFC) Panels

APFC panels switch capacitor steps to manage power factor against a configured target. The achievable result, utility billing effect and switching method depend on measured loads, harmonics, the approved design and the applicable tariff.

  • ✓ Microprocessor APFC relay controller with THD protection
  • ✓ Heavy-duty MPP capacitors & detuned harmonic reactors
  • ✓ Real-time power quality logging via Tor Titan MFM

Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) & Combined Distribution Panels

ATS panels ensure uninterrupted power supply by automatically transferring load between primary grid utility power and backup diesel generators upon mains failure, and switching back when mains power stabilizes.

  • ✓ Motorized 4-Pole ACBs / Changeover switches with electrical interlocks
  • ✓ Adjustable mains sensing voltage/frequency delay timers
  • ✓ Combined ATS cum Main Distribution panel enclosures

Busbar Distribution & Extended Busbar Trunking Panels

High-current electrolytic copper or aluminum busbar assemblies engineered for efficient power trunking across factory floors and riser shafts. Fully insulated with heat-shrinkable PVC/SMC shrouds and high-strength busbar supports.

Distribution Boards (DB) & Meter Panels

Compact indoor distribution boards and commercial multi-tenant energy meter distribution panels equipped with MCBs, RCCBs, and isolators.

Feeder Pillar & Outdoor Weatherproof Panels

IP55/IP65 rated outdoor weather-proof feeder pillars for industrial campuses, residential townships, and highway power distribution.

Fire Pump & Temperature Control Panels

Dedicated fire safety pump control panels compliant with NBC norms, and temperature control panels integrated with Tor Saturn controllers.

Which types of electrical panels does SS Exim manufacture?
