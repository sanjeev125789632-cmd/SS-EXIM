Heavy Duty Lighting

Energy-Efficient Illumination for High-Ceiling & Outdoor Bays

Industrial facilities require high-intensity, glare-free illumination that consumes minimal power and withstands continuous 24/7 operation. SS Exim supplies and installs heavy-duty LED High-Bay lights and outdoor LED Floodlights engineered for manufacturing floors, steel fabricators, chemical plants, and logistics parks.

Featuring extruded aluminum thermal heatsinks, high-efficiency Meanwell / Philips drivers, and toughened glass optics, our luminaires deliver up to 60% energy savings over traditional metal-halide fixtures while integrating easily into IBMS smart lighting automation.

Frequently Asked Questions — Industrial Lighting

What wattage of LED high bay is required for a 10-meter ceiling?

For a 10-meter warehouse or factory ceiling height, a 150W to 200W LED high bay (140 lm/W) typically delivers the recommended 200–300 Lux on the floor level.

Can these lights withstand voltage fluctuations on industrial grids?

Yes. Our industrial fixtures feature wide operating voltage drivers (100V to 300V AC) with built-in surge protection (up to 10kV), protecting against voltage spikes.

Do you supply automated street light & lighting feeder panels?

Yes. SS Exim custom fabricates outdoor lighting feeder panels equipped with astronomical timers, photocells, and contactors for automated yard lighting control.
