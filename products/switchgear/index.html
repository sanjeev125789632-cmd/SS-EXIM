Industrial Switchgear Supply & Distribution

Authorized supply of Air Circuit Breakers (ACB), Moulded Case Circuit Breakers (MCCB), and Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB) from Schneider, ABB, Siemens, Havells, L&T, and NOARK.

Authorized Distributor

Authentic Low-Voltage Protection & Switching Devices

Electrical protection is the bedrock of industrial safety. SS Exim is an authorized distributor and supply partner for top-tier global and Indian switchgear brands: Schneider Electric, ABB, Siemens, Havells, L&T Electrical, and NOARK.

We supply complete ranges of low-voltage circuit breakers—including heavy-duty Air Circuit Breakers (ACB) for main power incomers, Moulded Case Circuit Breakers (MCCB) for feeder distribution, and Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB) for branch circuit protection—serving panel builders, electrical contractors, and industrial plants across Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, and all major cities in India.

Circuit Protection Categories

Explore Our Switchgear Lineup

Air Circuit Breakers (ACB)

High-current incomer protection from 630A up to 6300A. Available in 3-Pole and 4-Pole draw-out / fixed execution from Schneider Masterpact, ABB Emax 2, Siemens 3WL, L&T C-Power, and NOARK Ex9A series.

Moulded Case Circuit Breakers (MCCB)

Feeder protection from 16A to 1600A with thermal-magnetic or electronic microprocessor trip units. High breaking capacity (25kA to 100kA) from Schneider NSX, ABB Tmax, Siemens 3VA, and L&T DN series.

Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB) & RCCBs

Final distribution protection from 0.5A to 125A in B, C, and D trip curves. Includes Residual Current Circuit Breakers (RCCB) for human shock protection from Schneider Acti9, ABB pro M, Havells, and Siemens 5SL.
