# SS-EXIM technical content cluster plan

These are editorial briefs, not approved engineering content. No calculator, worked example or technical article should be published until its inputs, calculations and sources are reviewed for the intended application.

## 1. APFC panel sizing calculator with a worked example

- Search intent: Informational/tool; estimate initial capacitor compensation and understand required inputs.
- Target audience: Plant electrical engineers, consultants, facility managers and purchase teams.
- Proposed title: APFC Panel Sizing Calculator: Inputs, Formula and Reviewed Example
- URL slug: `/tools/apfc-panel-sizing-calculator/`
- Main keyword: APFC panel sizing calculator
- Supporting questions: Which measured kW and power factor should be used? How are steps selected? When are detuned reactors required? How do harmonics affect sizing?
- Required technical inputs: Measured peak kW/kVA, initial and target PF, load profile, voltage/frequency, harmonic survey, transformer data, tariff requirements and switching duty.
- Required primary sources: Applicable IEC 60831 series, IEC/IS 61439 records, current utility tariff/order and selected capacitor/controller manufacturer manuals.
- Internal links: `/products/panels/`, `/blog/apfc-panel-guide/`, `/contact/`.
- CTA: Request review of measured data and an APFC panel quotation.
- Client approval: Calculation method, safety disclaimer, reviewed example inputs/result, engineering reviewer identity and offered scope.
