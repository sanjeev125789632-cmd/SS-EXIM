About SS Exim

Engineering smart building management systems, custom power panels, and industrial electrical distribution for commercial and industrial enterprises across India.

Who We Are

Your Trusted Partner in Power Engineering & IoT Intelligence

SS Exim is an electrical products supplier, IoT & Building Management Systems (BMS) integrator and custom panel builder with a registered address in Thane. Mumbai, Pune and Bangalore are service areas subject to project scope.

We provide electrical and automation products from brands including Tor.AI, Schneider Electric, ABB, Siemens, Havells, L&T and NOARK, subject to product availability and applicable OEM terms. Brand references identify requested or supplied product families and do not imply official representation.

Our Core Capabilities

Integrated Electrical & Automation Expertise

📡

IoT & BMS Integration

supplier and integrator of Tor.AI products, subject to availability and applicable OEM terms, integrating smart energy meters (Tor Titan), IoT telemetry gateways (Tor Jupiter, Venus, Pluto), and programmable controllers (Tor Saturn) for IBMS 4.0 automation.

⚡

Custom Panel Building

In-house fabrication of low-voltage distribution panels, MCC panels, APFC power factor correction panels, ATS main-generator switchover panels, busbar trunking, and distribution boards.

🏢

Switchgear & Brand Supply

Supplier of Air Circuit Breakers (ACB), Moulded Case Circuit Breakers (MCCB), and Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB) from leading brands, subject to product availability and applicable OEM terms.
