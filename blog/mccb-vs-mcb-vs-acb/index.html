Introduction to Low-Voltage Circuit Breakers

Selecting the correct protective circuit breaker is vital to safeguarding personnel, preventing electrical fires, and avoiding catastrophic equipment damage. When designing custom electrical distribution panels, engineers primarily choose among three distinct classes of circuit breakers: Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB), Moulded Case Circuit Breakers (MCCB), and Air Circuit Breakers (ACB).

While all three devices interrupt abnormal overload and short-circuit currents, their current ratings, breaking capacities, and tripping mechanisms differ significantly.

1. Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB)

MCBs are compact protective devices commonly used for final and sub-circuits. Available current ratings, breaking capacities and trip curves vary by product series and must be checked against the manufacturer datasheet and the project study.

  • Current Rating (In): Product-specific; verify the selected series
  • Short-Circuit Breaking Capacity (Icn): Must exceed the project requirement and match the applicable standard
  • Tripping Mechanism: Verify the curve and technology for the selected product
  • Key Benefit: Din-rail mounting, compact size, cost-effective

2. Moulded Case Circuit Breakers (MCCB)

MCCBs are robust, industrial-grade breakers enclosed in a heavy glass-filled polyester housing. They serve as primary protection for motor feeders, sub-distribution panels, and APFC panels.

  • Current Rating (In): Product-specific; verify the selected frame and trip unit
  • Short-Circuit Breaking Capacity (Icu): Select from the calculated fault level and exact datasheet
  • Tripping Mechanism: Adjustable thermal-magnetic or microprocessor electronic trip units
  • Key Benefit: Adjustable trip setpoints, auxiliary contacts, shunt trip options

3. Air Circuit Breakers (ACB)

ACBs are heavy-duty, high-capacity circuit breakers used as main incomer protection directly downstream of distribution transformers or diesel generators. They operate in air at atmospheric pressure and feature advanced arc chutes.

  • Current Rating (In): Product-specific; verify the selected frame and trip unit
  • Short-Circuit Breaking Capacity (Icu): Select from the calculated fault level, withstand requirement and exact datasheet
  • Execution: Fixed or Draw-out 3-Pole / 4-Pole
  • Key Benefit: Full selectivity (LSI/LSIG protection), motorized charging, remote tripping
