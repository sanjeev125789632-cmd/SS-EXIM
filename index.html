Technical FAQ

Electrical Panels, BMS and Project Support

General guidance for planning an enquiry. Final equipment selection and engineering decisions require project-specific assessment.

What is the difference between BMS and IBMS?

A BMS monitors and controls building services such as HVAC, lighting and energy metering. An IBMS connects several building systems through a common integration layer so operators can view coordinated alarms, trends and controls. The practical difference depends on system boundaries, protocols and the integration scope defined for the facility.

How much does an APFC panel cost in India?

APFC panel pricing cannot be stated responsibly without a specification. Cost depends on the required kVAr rating, number of steps, contactor or thyristor duty, capacitors and reactors, incomer, enclosure, controls, selected brands, testing requirements and installation scope. Submit the load data and project requirements for a technical quotation.

Which MCCB rating is suitable for an LT panel?

An MCCB must not be selected from load current alone. The engineer must assess load, system voltage, prospective fault level, cable capacity, starting current, protective coordination, breaking capacity and applicable standards. Trip settings and upstream or downstream protection also matter. Final selection requires a qualified electrical engineer and project-specific calculations.

What information is required for a custom electrical panel quotation?

Provide the single-line diagram, load schedule, system voltage and frequency, fault level, incomer and feeder ratings, busbar requirements, enclosure and IP requirements, cable entry, metering and control needs, preferred makes, applicable standards, testing requirements, site conditions and installation scope. Missing inputs should be confirmed before design or pricing.

Does SS-EXIM provide installation and commissioning?

SS-EXIM lists engineering, installation and commissioning among its services. Availability and responsibility depend on the equipment, project location, approved scope, site readiness and commercial terms. Confirm whether unloading, cabling, testing, energisation, documentation, training and ongoing maintenance are included when requesting a technical quotation.

Which cities does SS-EXIM serve?

SS-EXIM identifies Mumbai, Pune and Bangalore as service areas, with its registered address in Thane, Maharashtra. These locations should not be interpreted as staffed branch offices. Support in a particular city depends on the product, engineering requirement, project schedule and agreed scope. Confirm site-service availability when submitting an enquiry.
